Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The "Cosmic Questions Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of The Cosmic Questions YouTube channel to upload and schedule that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly to upload videos and to confirm it is connected to the correct channel. It does not access data of any other user.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
